# Ikonika Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Ikonika is a vocabulary trainer for Android and Windows. This policy explains what data the app
handles, where that data goes, and how to delete it.

Ikonika has no servers of its own. The developer never receives your words, your progress, your
account details or anything about your device.

## Data on your device

Your words, review progress, settings, reading library and session state are stored on your
device. Without Google sign-in, this data never leaves the device.

## Google sign-in and sync

Signing in is optional. The app works fully without it.

When you sign in with Google, Ikonika requests access to two things:

- Your email address and name, to show which account is signed in.
- Access to a hidden app folder in your Google Drive. Ikonika stores a copy of your words and
  progress there, plus any books you choose to upload, so that your devices stay in sync. The app
  cannot see or change any other file in your Drive, and other apps cannot see this folder.

The data goes straight from your device to Google. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone
else.

Ikonika's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Downloads

Dictionaries, voices, free books and update information download from GitHub, a public file host.
Downloads need no account and send no personal data. Like any website, GitHub sees your device's
IP address when it serves a file. See the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Sentence translation on Android

On Android, Ikonika translates sentences with Google ML Kit. Translation runs on your device, and
the text you translate never leaves it. The language models download from Google. ML Kit may also
send Google device information, app information and usage metrics for the translation feature.
See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Links to other sites

Some buttons open other websites, for example a dictionary site to look up the word you are
studying. Those sites receive the word from the address they are opened with, and their own
privacy policies apply.

## Notifications

Review reminders are scheduled on your device. No server sends them, and no data leaves your
device for them.

## What Ikonika does not do

- No advertising and no ad identifiers.
- No analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
- No accounts of its own. Google sign-in is the only account involved.
- No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.

## Deleting your data

Settings, Data, Delete everything removes your words and progress from the device, and from the
synced copy in Google Drive at the next sync.

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the device.

To remove the synced copy from Google Drive entirely, open Google Drive settings, choose Manage
apps, find Ikonika, and choose Delete hidden app data. To stop the app's access, remove Ikonika
from your [Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).

## Children

Ikonika is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
